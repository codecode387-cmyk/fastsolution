The ritual

How to get the best results

Two easy steps: scrub in the shower, then moisturise while your skin is still soft.

Body Wash with Scrub

Start with 2 to 3 times a week, then adjust to how your skin feels.

  1. Wet your skinStep into the shower and wet your body thoroughly.
  2. Lather upSqueeze a generous amount onto your hands or a loofah and rub to a rich lather.
  3. Massage gentlyUse slow circular motions over your body so the beads can buff away dead skin.
  4. Rinse and pat dryRinse well with warm water and pat skin dry.

Body Massage Lotion

Use daily, ideally right after your shower while skin is slightly damp.

  1. PumpDispense a small amount into your palm.
  2. MassageWarm it between your hands and rub gently onto your skin in circular motions.
  3. AbsorbKeep massaging until it is fully absorbed, then feel the smoothness.
Patch test firstIf your skin is sensitive, try a small area first. Stop using a product if irritation occurs.
