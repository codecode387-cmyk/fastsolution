How We Deliver

To Your Door in Lagos. Across Nigeria by Waybill.

Two ways to move your package, both handled by one team. Here is exactly what happens from the moment you send your request to the moment it is delivered.

Doorstep Delivery in Lagos State

Picked up from you, handed to the receiver.

Anywhere within Lagos State, our riders and drivers collect from your shop, home or supplier and take the package straight to the receiver's door. No dropping it at a park for them to find.

  1. 1

    Send us the details

    Message us on WhatsApp or use the quote form: what you are sending, pickup address, delivery address and the receiver's phone number.

  2. 2

    Agree the price

    We confirm the delivery fee with you before we move. The price depends on the distance and the size or weight of the package.

  3. 3

    We pick up

    A rider comes to your location at the agreed time and checks the package with you before leaving.

  4. 4

    We call the receiver

    Before arrival, the rider contacts the receiver so someone is ready to take the package.

  5. 5

    Delivered and confirmed

    The package is handed over at the door and we confirm completion with you.

Waybill Across Nigeria

Documented from Lagos to the destination state.

Sending to another state? Your package travels with a waybill, the document that records what is being shipped, who sent it and who will receive it. That record keeps every package traceable from pickup to arrival.

  1. 1

    Tell us the destination

    Give us the state and town, the receiver's name and phone number, and a description of the items.

  2. 2

    We quote and confirm

    The fee is based on the destination, weight and size. You agree it before anything is dispatched.

  3. 3

    Pickup and packing check

    We collect the package or you drop it off. We check it is packed well enough for a long journey, and seal it.

  4. 4

    Waybill is issued

    We record the package details and give you the waybill information so you can follow up on the shipment.

  5. 5

    Transit and arrival

    The package travels to the destination state. The receiver is notified when it arrives, and we confirm completion with you.

Have these ready and we move faster

A complete request means a quicker pickup and fewer calls back and forth.

  • ✓Receiver's name and working phone number. Riders call before arrival.
  • ✓A clear address with a landmark. It saves time on the last stretch.
  • ✓What is inside, and roughly how heavy. Pricing depends on size and weight.
  • ✓Fragile items packed securely. Tell us if anything needs extra care.
